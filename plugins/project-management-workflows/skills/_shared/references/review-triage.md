# Review Triage

Turn material findings into backlog work only when the finding is durable,
actionable, and scoped tightly enough to review.

Default issue kinds:

- `Bug`: broken behavior or clear regression risk
- `Maintenance`: cleanup, refactor, CI, or developer-experience work
- `Enhancement`: product or capability work
- `Research`: open design or decision question
- `Epic`: large or complex multi-issue workstream that should be broken down before review and implementation

Use concise, decision-oriented titles and bodies.

Recommended issue-body sections:

- Goal
- Why now
- Current baseline
- In scope
- Out of scope
- Acceptance criteria or decision output
